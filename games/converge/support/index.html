Duskore Converge — Game support

Converge support

Support for the iOS app and prerelease testing. Release preparation is in progress; this page does not claim TestFlight availability, completed service setup or App Store approval.

Playing

After sign-in, tap PLAY. Tap an empty board cell to pull the nearest tile from each of four directions toward it. Matching groups of three or more that include a pulled tile pop. The empty point connects its neighbors for matching. Two tiles arrive after a pull and one every five seconds. HOME ends the run and returns to the menu; PLAY AGAIN starts a new run after the board fills or no legal pull remains. Best scores are local, not an online leaderboard.

Coins and ads

Five daily play coins are provided; a new run costs one. When available and permitted, an optional rewarded ad grants one play coin after confirmed completion. A separate bottom banner may appear; no interstitial ads are used. Declining tracking does not prevent play with available coins. If no ad is available, try later or wait for daily coins.

Accounts and privacy choices

Guest, Google and Apple sign-in depend on their configured services. Menu Settings offers sign-out and Delete Account. Successful deletion removes the Firebase account and clears this account's local coins and local best, then returns to login without automatically opening provider sign-in. Apple authorization revocation is part of the Apple deletion path. PRIVACY CHOICES opens available regional ad choices; system tracking settings can be revisited. Do not post private account data in support issues.

Sound and reports

Shared sound controls manage effects and the game-specific Magnetic Bloom background music included in the release candidate. This is not an App Store availability announcement. For a bug, include the game name, exact version/build, device, OS and reproduction steps. Never post passwords, tokens or private screenshots.
