Duskore Games — Privacy policy

Scope and date

Updated October 17, 2026. This policy covers the prerelease Duskore Games iOS collection (com.duskore.games), including its nine embedded games. Existing standalone apps have separate accounts, data and policies. The support website has no app analytics or advertising scripts; GitHub's hosting and public-issue policies also apply.

Accounts and local information

Firebase Authentication and Google/Apple sign-in process account identifiers and provider information; authorized providers may supply email, name or profile details. Guest access uses an anonymous Firebase account. Favorites, recent games, scores, play coins, game state and settings are stored on the device. Coins are not purchases or currency. Existing standalone accounts/saves are not automatically migrated and no cloud save backup is promised.

Scores and sharing

Hexa Merge, Star Jumper, Stack and Pop and 2048 can use Apple Game Center after authentication and explicit score-sharing consent. Submitted scores and Game Center player information follow Apple's service rules. Declining sharing prevents score submission. Other games use local scores. Deleting the Firebase collection account does not delete the Apple Game Center account or necessarily remove previously submitted Game Center records.

Analytics and diagnostics

Firebase Analytics may process app/game events, installation identifiers, device/OS/app information, language and approximate region. Crashlytics may process crash information, installation identifiers and device/OS/app details. Analytics advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalization are separately denied by default and do not become enabled through AdMob consent.

Ads and choices

Google AdMob and UMP support bottom banners and optional rewarded ads. This implementation checks iOS tracking permission and regional consent before requesting ads. Advertising partners may process device/advertising identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics; with applicable permission information may be combined across other companies' apps and websites for measurement or personalization. Regional choices may address processing considered sale or sharing. You may decline tracking and continue with available coins. PRIVACY CHOICES and iOS Settings provide ways to revisit available choices.

Retention and deletion

Settings - Delete Account requests Firebase account deletion and cleanup of collection-local data, including unopened games; the Apple path includes authorization revocation. Provider cancellation or errors do not count as success. Local storage failure may require further cleanup and support. Sign-out or uninstall alone does not delete a server account. Analytics, diagnostic, advertising and Game Center records follow provider retention and legal requirements and may not be directly linked to the deleted Firebase account. No deletion of standalone-app accounts or your Apple/Google account is implied.

Providers and contact

Services include Firebase Authentication/Analytics/Crashlytics, Google and Apple sign-in, Apple Game Center, AdMob/UMP and disclosed advertising partners, and GitHub for public support. Providers may process data internationally under their safeguards. This is a general-audience collection, not specifically directed to children. Contact support for privacy questions without posting identifying data publicly. Public issues remain until removed by their author, GitHub or an administrator. Material changes update this policy's date.
