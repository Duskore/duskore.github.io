Same Day Appraiser — Privacy policy

Scope and date

Same Day Appraiser privacy policy. Updated 2026-11-11. This policy applies to Duskore’s offline iPhone and iPad game.

No personal data collection

The app has no accounts, login, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or network requests. It does not collect personal information or track users.

Data stored on your device

Progress, settings and judgment records are written only to the app’s storage on your device. Judgment records are gameplay CSV files containing details such as the day, item, stamp and elapsed time. These files are not sent anywhere. There is no cloud backup, and deleting the app deletes this local data.

License links

License links in Credits open in the system browser only when you choose to tap them. The linked websites follow their own policies.

Support website

This support website contains no analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub hosting and public support issues follow GitHub’s policies. Anything you post in a public issue is visible to others; do not include personal information.

Audience and questions

The game is for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. For privacy questions, use the support link without posting personal information. Material policy changes will update the date above.
