Duskore Sudoku support

This page supports the iOS app, including prerelease testing. The app is being prepared for release; a public download is not yet announced.

Playing and continuing

Sign in, select a difficulty and press PLAY. Fill each row, column and 3×3 box with the digits 1–9 without repetition. Three mistakes end the attempt. PLAY AGAIN resets the same puzzle; NEW GAME selects another. HOME returns to the menu, where CONTINUE restores your unfinished puzzle on this device. Progress is local, not a cloud backup.

Hints and advertising

A puzzle starts with one hint. When advertising is enabled and available, you can choose a rewarded ad for another hint or one-strike recovery. Rewards require the ad's reward confirmation. A banner may appear above gameplay. Declining tracking keeps the game playable without ads; rewarded benefits are unavailable while ads are disabled. If an ad is unavailable, retry later or restart the puzzle.

Accounts and deletion

From the menu, open Settings → Delete Account and confirm. The app deletes the Firebase account and local unfinished puzzle, then returns to the login screen. Apple authorization revocation is attempted for Apple accounts. Some providers may require explicit reauthentication; cancelling does not complete deletion. Signing out alone does not delete your account. Uninstalling is not account deletion.

Privacy choices

Open PRIVACY CHOICES from the menu to inspect advertising status and revisit available regional choices. On iPhone, OPEN TRACKING SETTINGS opens the app's system settings when the native permission service is available. Returning to the app rechecks permission.

Report a problem

Use the public GitHub support link below. Include “Duskore Sudoku”, the app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version and reproduction steps. Do not post passwords, tokens, email addresses, Apple/Google account details or screenshots containing private information. If in-app account deletion fails, report the error without identifying account data.
