Duskore Mirror Fold — Privacy policy

Scope and date

Mirror Fold privacy policy, updated October 7, 2026. Duskore provides this prerelease iOS puzzle app. The support website has no app analytics or ad scripts; GitHub hosting and public issues follow GitHub's policies. Publishing this policy does not establish release readiness.

Authentication and local data

Firebase Authentication processes account identifiers and provider information for Guest, Google and Apple sign-in. Providers may supply email, name or profile information with your authorization. Guest sign-in creates an anonymous Firebase account. Play coins, local best scores, theme and sound settings are stored on the device. Coins are not purchases or currency with monetary value. There is no cloud score backup or Game Center sharing in this version.

Analytics and diagnostics

Firebase Analytics may process app sessions, game events, installation identifiers, app/device/OS information, language and approximate region for usage analysis. Crashlytics may process crash details, installation identifiers, timestamps, device/OS and app version for diagnostics. Analytics advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalization are denied separately and are not enabled by granting AdMob permission. Authentication, analytics and diagnostics are distinct from advertising tracking permission.

Advertising and choices

AdMob and UMP provide bottom banner and optional rewarded advertising. On iOS the app requires tracking permission before entering its advertising consent flow, then checks regional consent and whether ads may be requested. Ad providers may process device and advertising identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostic data. With applicable permission, data may be combined with information from other companies' apps or websites for advertising measurement or personalization. Regional choices may cover processing regarded as sale or sharing. You can decline tracking and keep playing with available coins, and revisit PRIVACY CHOICES or system settings.

Providers, retention and deletion

Providers include Google Firebase Authentication, Analytics and Crashlytics; Google Sign-In; Apple Sign in with Apple; Google AdMob/UMP and disclosed ad partners; and GitHub for public support. They may process data outside your country under their safeguards. Settings -> Delete Account removes the Firebase account and clears the account's local play coins and local best; Apple authorization revocation is included in the Apple path. Other device preferences may remain until app data is removed. Analytics, crash and ad records follow provider retention and legal obligations and may not be directly linked to the deleted account. App deletion or sign-out alone does not delete the server account. Deleting this app account does not delete your Apple or Google account.

Contact and audience

This is a general-audience puzzle game, not specifically directed to children. Use the support link for privacy or deletion problems without posting identifying account data publicly. Public support issues remain until removed by their author, GitHub or an administrator. Material changes will update this policy's date.
