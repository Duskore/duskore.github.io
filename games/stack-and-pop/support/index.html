Stack & Pop — Game support

Duskore Stack & Pop support

For the iOS app and prerelease testing. The app is in development; no public download or release date is announced. These pages do not establish TestFlight device testing or App Store approval.

Play and daily coins

Tap to place the moving block on the stack. A miss costs one of three lives; the run ends when no lives remain. Sign in and select PLAY to start. A successfully started new round uses one play coin; HOME returns to the menu without an extra charge. The initial allowance is five free coins per local calendar day, with no rollover. Coins and settings are stored on this device, not synchronized across devices. Coins are not sold and have no cash value.

Optional advertising

A banner may appear in reserved space outside the game controls. WATCH AD · +1 PLAY COIN is optional and grants one coin only after the advertising SDK confirms the earned reward. Closing, cancelling or a failed ad does not grant a coin. No interstitial ads are used. Advertising requires iOS tracking authorization and applicable regional permission. When advertising is unavailable or declined, daily free coins remain available; after they are used, wait for the next daily reset. Dedicated advertising QA builds use test ads; production candidates use production units, which may be unavailable before public release.

Account and privacy controls

Guest, Google and Apple sign-in depend on their services being configured and available. From the menu, open Settings to sign out or select Delete Account. Successful account deletion returns to the login screen without automatically starting a new provider login. Apple account deletion includes authorization revocation and may require explicit reauthentication; cancelling does not complete deletion. Signing out or uninstalling is not server account deletion. PRIVACY CHOICES shows tracking/ad status and any available regional choices; system tracking permission can be changed in iOS Settings.

Testing and support

Report Duskore Stack & Pop, app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version and reproduction steps using the public GitHub support link below. Do not post passwords, tokens, emails, account identifiers or screenshots with private information. The current candidate includes background music and optional Game Center leaderboards. Use SCORE SHARING to allow or stop future score submissions; game play does not require sharing. Sound controls are in Settings.
